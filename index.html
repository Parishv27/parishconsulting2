The Problem

Districts do not lack plans. They lack alignment between data, instruction, and leadership.

The binder is on the shelf. The data meetings happen on schedule. And the scores stay flat. When an improvement plan stalls, the cost compounds quietly, year over year, and the students who can least afford another flat year absorb the loss.

The Approach

The Signature 3-Phase Impact Model

A named, sequential, repeatable method. Your district always knows what phase it is in, what happens next, and what it receives.

1

Assess

Deeply analyze school data, instructional practices, and organizational systems.

Your district receives: a clear diagnostic built from classroom walkthroughs and data analysis, with a debrief that names what is working and what is not.

2

Align

Develop targeted, strategic plans highly tailored to your district's specific goals.

Your district receives: a strategic plan with priorities, owners, and timelines, aligned to your goals rather than a template.

3

Accelerate

Implement responsive solutions with ongoing executive coaching and staff support.

Your district receives: sustained implementation support through executive coaching cycles, professional development, and progress monitoring.

Questions districts ask, answered

What does Parish Educational Consulting do?

Parish Educational Consulting, LLC partners with K-12 school districts to improve instruction, develop leaders, and raise student achievement. Its five core service areas are instructional leadership and school improvement, leadership development, student achievement and intervention, school culture and climate, and alternative and nontraditional education consulting.

Who is Dr. Veronica Williams Parish?

Dr. Veronica Williams Parish is the founder and lead consultant of Parish Educational Consulting. She is a former school principal, district leader, and assistant professor whose leadership record includes improved student outcomes and documented gains in student and staff attendance, teacher morale, and family engagement, along with significant reductions in discipline incidents and out-of-school suspensions. Her expertise spans K-12, higher education, and correctional education systems.

What is the 3-Phase Impact Model?

The 3-Phase Impact Model is Parish Educational Consulting's signature method: Assess (deeply analyze school data, instructional practices, and organizational systems), Align (develop targeted strategic plans tailored to the district's goals), and Accelerate (implement responsive solutions with ongoing executive coaching and staff support).

Where does Parish Educational Consulting work?

The practice is based in Mississippi and serves school districts across the state and surrounding region, with national engagements available by arrangement. Professional development and coaching are delivered on campus in your district.

How much does an education consultant cost?

Parish engagements range from $500 to $700 for a single walkthrough and debrief visit, up to $2,500 to $4,500 per month for a full 6 to 12 month school improvement partnership. Professional development runs $700 to $1,000 per session, and a multi-session series runs $2,500 to $5,000.

What does a principal coaching cycle cost?

Monthly leadership coaching for principals and assistant principals runs $1,500 to $2,500 per month, per leader, typically structured as a 3 to 6 month cycle.

Can an engagement be customized to my district?

Yes. Every engagement is scoped to the district, from a half-day session to a year-long partnership, and fully customized support packages are available.

Has Dr. Veronica Williams Parish written any books?

Yes. Dr. Parish is the author of four books available on Amazon in Kindle and paperback editions: Knowing My History: For Black Youths (ages 8 to 13, with a teacher and parent guide), Black Man, Black Man, Where Are You? (a story of fatherhood, incarceration, and redemption), Oh, How I look Up to my Dad!, and Grandpa Curtis Lets Me Do Whatever I Want!
