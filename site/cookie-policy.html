How this website uses cookies

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy explains how parisheducationalconsulting.com (the "website"), operated by Parish Educational Consulting, LLC, uses cookies and similar technologies. It describes what the website actually loads and stores, so you can make an informed choice.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device. Cookies are commonly used to remember preferences, keep you signed in, or measure how a site is used. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work the same way for the purposes of this policy.

Cookies this website sets

This website does not use advertising cookies, and it does not use analytics or tracking cookies. The only cookie it stores is the one that remembers your own cookie choice, so the consent banner does not reappear on every page.

CookieCategoryPurpose
Cookie consent preference Strictly necessary Records whether you accepted or declined cookies, so your choice is respected on later visits and the banner is not shown again.

Strictly necessary cookies are required for the website to function and for your privacy choice to be honored. Under most privacy laws they do not require consent, but they are listed here for transparency.

Third-party services the website loads

Some files are loaded from other companies' servers. These do not set cookies on this website, but requesting a file from another server does reveal your IP address and browser details to that company, so they are disclosed here.

The cookie consent tool itself is hosted on this website's own server, so displaying the banner does not send any request to a third party.

Links to other websites

This website links to Amazon product pages for Dr. Parish's books. If you follow one of those links, Amazon's own cookie and privacy practices apply once you are on their site. This policy does not cover them.

Changing your choice

You can change your decision at any time by clearing this website's cookies in your browser, which will cause the consent banner to appear again on your next visit. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies entirely through their settings, though blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent your privacy choice from being remembered.
